    What can go wrong

    • The painting can lose value. Shares and the credit market move with its valuation, and a share price in the pool can fall below the valuation.
    • Selling can be slow. Only a small amount of liquidity sits in the pool, so large sales move the price.
    • Borrowing against shares can end in liquidation. If the valuation falls or you borrow close to the limit, your shares can be sold to repay the loan.
    • Lenders depend on the valuation and on the sale of seized shares. A loan is covered by shares in one painting, not by cash.
    • Only verified wallets can hold or trade the shares. Losing access to a verified wallet can lock your position.
    • Smart contracts can contain errors. These are new contracts that have not had the track record of older protocols.
